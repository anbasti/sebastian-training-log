Recovery guardrails

Keep the plan useful.

Start with 2–3 reps in reserve

For the first two weeks, leave two to three good reps in reserve on most sets.

Use 100-style finishers sparingly

Use one isolation-exercise finisher every 7–10 days only. Do not use it for squats, Romanian deadlifts, heavy pressing, or rows.

Adjust Friday when recovery is poor

After under 6 hours of sleep, elevated resting heart rate, or unusual fatigue, choose easy walking and mobility instead.

Stop for warning signs

Stop for sharp joint pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, severe breathlessness, severe muscle pain, or dark urine. Seek urgent medical care for severe symptoms.
